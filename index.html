I love you 100%

My dearest love,

One hundred days. It feels like a heartbeat and like a lifetime all at once. Every one of those days, you have quietly rewritten what happiness means to me.

I counted one hundred roses for you, one for every morning I woke up grateful that you exist. One for your laugh that makes the whole room softer. One for the way you look at me as if I were someone worth looking at. And all the rest for the countless small moments I never want to forget.

Before you, I thought love was a word people used too easily. Now I know it is the warmth in my chest when your name lights up my phone, the calm that falls over me when you hold my hand, the ache when we are apart even for a single evening. I am hopelessly, deeply, beautifully yours.

I love your strength and your gentleness. I love your sleepy voice and your stubborn little smile. I love the way you make ordinary days feel like poetry. You are my favorite hello and my hardest goodbye.

I promise to love you with my whole heart, not only on the easy days, but on the tired ones, the messy ones, the days when the world feels heavy. I promise to listen, to choose you again and again, to be your home and your adventure. I promise to grow old with you in the same stubborn, tender way that I fell: completely.

These hundred roses will fade, but my love for you will not. It will bloom a little more with every sunrise, every year, every chapter we write together. Today is only the first hundred. I want all the rest of them with you.

I love you, not just today, but forever, with every beat of my heart. I love you 100%, and then some.

Forever yours ♡
